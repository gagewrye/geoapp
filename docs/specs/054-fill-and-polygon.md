# 054: Fill and Polygon tools

**Status:** Accepted (2026-11-11). Proposed by Rahual (2026-11-05); Gage accepted it as built, with the answers below as implemented. **Amended by spec 059:** a fill also fills the holes of at most 2 px its region encloses, so scope 2's region and criterion 3's `floodFill` are no longer a plain flood fill. **ADRs:** none (no dependency; ADR 0009's hand-rolled brush pattern is followed). **Builds on specs 003, 014, 023, 033, 037, 046 and 049.** Reverses part of ADR 0009's Context ("no polygon labelling is left"): polygons come back as a way to fill pixels, not as vector labels.

**Request** (Rahual, 2026-11-05, correcting the 8-class pseudo-labels on the drone chunks): "add fill tool and polygon tool". Correcting a mask means recolouring whole blobs and outlining large regions; with the brush alone both are slow.

## Open questions (recommended answers are what the code does)
1. **Keys.** `F` for Fill and `L` for Polygon (`P` is Pan). Alternative for Polygon: `G`.
2. **Fill's reach.** A fill reads at most the clicked tile and 4 tiles each way (81 tiles, about 1000 px from the click), then stops and says so; a second click continues. A larger reach costs more reads and undo memory per click (ADR 0015's 32 MiB).
3. **Polygon size.** Corners span at most 2048 px each way (81 tiles). A click that would exceed it is refused with a message and the outline is kept.
4. **Right click in Polygon** removes the last corner (Escape discards the outline). Spec 049 made a right click discard Smart's whole selection; a long outline is more work to lose.
5. **Connectivity.** Fill is 4-connected, so it doesn't leak through diagonal gaps.

**Goal:** In Fill mode a click gives the connected region under it the selected class; in Polygon mode clicked corners outline a region that Enter fills. Each is one undoable edit.

## Scope
1. **Modes.** `BrushMode` gains `"fill"` and `"polygon"`, with `#mode-fill` and `#mode-polygon` buttons between Erase and Smart, and keys `F` and `L`. They are disabled exactly when Paint is (spec 019), show hidden labels when chosen, and hiding labels selects Pan (spec 016). As in Smart mode, drags pan, a click is a press and release within 4 px on a raster cell, Alt+click picks (spec 038), and a Shift+left press is kept from MapLibre's box zoom. In these two modes double-click zoom is off and the cursor is a crosshair.
2. **Fill.** A click reads the clicked pixel's effective label (stored tile, else the mask's, else 0) and fills the valid pixels 4-connected to it that share that label, unlabelled included. Imagery nodata is never filled and stops the fill. Tiles are read one round at a time as the fill enters them (`floodFill`), within `FILL_REACH`. The result is one spec 023 `PaintOp` (spans, the selected value, `erase: false`) on a fresh `UndoHistory` stroke, ended at once, so base tiles are copied on touch (spec 014) and Ctrl+Z and redo work (spec 037). The message says `Filled <n> px.`, adding when the fill got as far as its reach that a part may be left to click (it doesn't read past the reach to find out). A click on the selected class says so and changes nothing; a click on nodata says there is no imagery. Clicks are ignored while a fill is read or applied, while strokes are still queued (their tiles are about to change), during a peek (spec 034) and while labels are locked (spec 004). Undo, Import labels and Export labels wait for a fill, as they do for the paint queue. Leaving the mode or opening a raster drops a fill still reading.
3. **Polygon.** Each click adds a corner at the clicked pixel's centre. The outline (corners, the area so far, and a line on to the pointer) is drawn in a new layer slot `polygon` between `smart` and `brush-hover`, in the selected class colour. Enter, a click within 8 screen px of the first corner, or a double click (a click within 8 px of the last corner) fills `polygonSpans` of the corners as one `PaintOp`, as Fill does; it needs 3 corners. A double click's second press never starts a new outline, even when the fill has already landed. Backspace or a right click removes the last corner. No corner is added during a peek. Escape discards the outline and keeps the tool; with no outline it selects Pan as before. Leaving the mode or opening a raster discards the outline. Enter does nothing while labels are locked or a button has focus.
4. **Status and help.** `#status-tool` shows `Fill · <class>` (with ` · reading…` while a fill runs) and `Polygon · <class>`; Smart's ` · reading…` shows only in Smart mode. `SHORTCUTS` gains `F`, `L`, Click (fill; add a corner), Enter / Double click, Backspace / Right click and Esc.

**Out of scope:** erasing with either tool; holes or multi-part polygons; moving a placed corner; a fill tolerance on imagery colour; fills past the reach in one click; vector output.

## Interfaces
- `core/fill.ts` (pure): `FILL_REACH = 4`. `fillTile(labels: Uint8Array | null, mask: ValidMask, t: Window, target: number, bits: Uint8Array, seeds: number[]): FillEdges` spreads inside one tile from seed indices, sets `bits`, and returns the edge rows and columns it newly filled. `edgeSeeds(dims, t, edges)` turns those into each neighbouring tile's window and seed indices, and `inReach(cell, x0, y0)` says whether a tile is within the reach.
- `core/polygon.ts` (pure): `POLYGON_MAX = 2048`. `polygonFits(corners, next): boolean`. `polygonSpans(corners: [number, number][], dims): Span[]`: pixels whose centre is inside (even-odd) plus the Bresenham cells of each edge, clipped, as disjoint sorted spans. `core/spans.ts` gains `mergeRows`, taken out of `sweptSpans` unchanged.
- `io/fill.ts`: `floodFill(dims, cell, value, read, stop?): Promise<FillResult | "nodata" | "same" | null>` and `fillReader(imagery, store, base, cache): FillRead` (stored tile, else `maskedBaseTile` with the cached valid mask).
- `map/polygonLayer.ts`, `ui/fill.ts`, `ui/polygon.ts`. `ui/clickTools.ts` owns Smart, Fill and Polygon for `ui/labels.ts`, which stays under 200 lines. `map/brush.ts` `Deps.click` and `Deps.discard` now fire in all three modes.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Unit (core):** `polygonSpans` of a rectangle's corners is exactly its pixels in either winding; a concave notch stays empty; a bow tie follows even-odd; for 40 random star polygons every pixel more than 1 px from the outline matches ray casting, and every corner is filled; output is clipped, disjoint and sorted. `polygonFits` allows a span of 2047 px and refuses 2048.
2. **Unit (core):** `fillTile` fills 4-connected pixels only, reports edge pixels per side, treats null labels as 0, stops at nodata, and on a second call keeps earlier bits and reports only new ones. `edgeSeeds` indexes into partial edge tiles correctly and gives nothing past the raster; `inReach` is true 4 tiles away and false 5.
3. **Unit (io):** `floodFill` equals a whole-raster flood fill on a 700 x 600 raster with partial edge tiles, for 5 cells. It reads only tiles the region reaches, each once and none larger than a tile. It returns `"nodata"` and `"same"` after one read, stops at `FILL_REACH` with `clipped` and exactly 81 reads, doesn't call a raster edge a clip, returns null once `stop` is true, and rejects on a failed read.
4. **Unit (io, real store, mask base, `MaskCache` and `PaintQueue`):** a fill of an unedited imported mask, then of the region around it (part stored, part base), gives the expected labels and stops at nodata; two undos leave no stored tile.
5. **Unit (ui/fill, ui/clickTools):** one op per click as one ended stroke; clicks are ignored while one reads or lands, while strokes are queued and while locked; `"same"`, `"nodata"` and failures push nothing and say why; cancel mid-read pushes nothing. In `clickTools`, a click reaches only the selected tool; leaving Fill or opening a raster drops a fill still reading; Undo, Import and Export are told when a fill starts and ends, and `labels.ts` gates `blocked` and `busy` on it; `reading` is false for a tool that isn't selected.
6. **Unit (ui/polygon):** corners, apply (one op of `polygonSpans`, once), closing on the first or last corner, back, discard, the 2048 px refusal, locked, a dropped op keeping the outline, and open. With a fill that lands inside `push`, a double click on the first or last corner leaves no corner, and a later or distant click starts a new outline.
7. **Unit (map/brush):** in Fill and Polygon a left click calls `click`, a drag or an off-raster click doesn't, a right click calls `discard`, and only double-click zoom is turned off; Paint still turns off all three handlers and Smart none. While blocked (a peek) Fill and Polygon take no click, and Smart's clicks are unchanged.
8. **Unit (ui/labelKeys):** `F` and `L` (blocked like `B`); Enter, Backspace and Escape reach the polygon and are prevented only when they acted; a pending smart selection takes Enter and Escape first.
9. **Unit:** `formatTool` and `SHORTCUTS` as in scope 4; spec 039 criterion 1 passes.
10. **E2E (imported 16 px block mask):** a Fill click recolours exactly one block, an unlabelled block fills, a click on the selected class changes nothing, and Undo and Redo work.
11. **E2E (nodata imagery):** a fill labels every valid pixel and none in the nodata block; a click on nodata labels nothing and leaves Undo disabled.
12. **E2E (nodata imagery):** four corners and Enter label `polygonSpans` minus nodata, and one Undo clears it. A right click and Backspace each remove a corner; a double click fills and the zoom is unchanged.
13. **E2E:** the buttons' `aria-pressed`, titles and disabled state, Escape (discard, then Pan), leaving the tool, hidden labels, and the `#help` rows.
14. `npm run check` passes apart from the 4 tests that fail on `main` at 5594d74 (README wording, and one Ctrl/Cmd title on macOS). Specs 033, 046 and 049's criteria pass.
15. **Docs:** README describes both tools. CLAUDE.md's `core/` line should gain "fills and polygons"; only Gage edits CLAUDE.md.
