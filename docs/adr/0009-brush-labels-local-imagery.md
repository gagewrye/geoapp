# 0009: The paint brush is the only labelling tool; terra-draw stays only for the AOI rectangle

**Status:** Accepted (Gage, 2026-10-26)

**Context:** Gage (2026-10-26) wants a paint brush in place of polygon drawing. He also decided that AlphaEarth is never hand-labelled. Labels are painted on local imagery and can train a model on AlphaEarth (spec 004), so no polygon labelling is left. terra-draw 1.38.0 draws vectors only. Its `terra-draw.ts` at tag `terra-draw@1.38.0` exports `TerraDrawRectangleMode`, which spec 002 uses for its AOI. It starts in a built-in `"static"` mode, and `setMode` stops the old mode before it starts the new one. maplibre-gl 6.16.0 (`src/ui/handler/mouse.ts`, `handler_manager.ts`, `keyboard.ts`): mouse pan is the left button without Ctrl, and rotate is the right button or Ctrl+left. `dragPan` wraps mouse pan and touch pan together. The mouse handlers listen for `mousedown` on the canvas container. The keyboard handler binds only `=`/`+`, `-` and the arrow keys. `transform.isPointOnMapSurface` tells whether a screen point is on the globe.

**Decision:** The brush is hand-rolled in `map/`, adds no dependency, and labels local imagery only. Polygon labelling is deleted, along with its code: `TerraDrawPolygonMode`, `TerraDrawSelectMode`, `checkRing` and its validations, `toPixelPolygons`, `samplePixels` and `PixelPolygon`. terra-draw is kept for spec 002's AOI rectangle. It stays in `"static"` mode except while the AOI tool is active, and during that time the brush is in Pan. In Paint mode, `map/` keeps mouse and pen `mousedown` from reaching MapLibre rather than calling `dragPan.disable()`, which would also stop touch pan. It also disables `dragRotate`, `doubleClickZoom` and `boxZoom`.

**Consequences:** No dependency is added or removed. ADR 0003 is partly superseded. Its choice of terra-draw holds for the AOI rectangle. Its ±180° polygon consequence lapses, because an AOI that crosses 180° spans two UTM zones and `aoiGrid` refuses it. ADR 0004's rule that vertices beyond `MAX_LAT` are blocked lapses too. Spec 001 criteria 2, 3, 5 and 13-15, and the polygon parts of 8 and 12, are superseded (spec 003). Brush code must normalize longitude. Dropping terra-draw later would mean hand-rolling the rectangle.
