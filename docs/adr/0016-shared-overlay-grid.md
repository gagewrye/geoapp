# 0016: Label overlays of one raster share one Mercator pixel grid, sized to the finest pixel

**Status:** Accepted (Gage, 2026-10-27, with spec 011); kept 2026-10-28 with its residual hairlines ("keep the grid as is with no gpu layer for now"); pitch rule replaced 2026-10-28 ("we need labelling precision, even if there is overlap. slow opening is fine"; "your recommendations are good"). Amends ADR 0010 (overlay resolution, index type, memory). Superseded by ADR 0019 (2026-10-29).

**Context:** ADR 0010 gives each label tile its own canvas, sampled on a Mercator grid fitted to that tile, so on grids rotated against Mercator neighbouring grids are offset and seams get gaps and double-opacity strips (spec 011). One grid for the raster (pixel edges at multiples of a pitch from Mercator (0, 0), each cell painted by the tile holding its centre) fixes this. Two pitch rules sized it to the largest tile, first the corner tiles, then all tiles, and both were rejected. The corner rule broke its 258 px bound (270 px on a middle row; about 910 px at ±`MAX_LAT`). The all-tiles rule draws tiles coarser than their raster pixels, losing labelling precision.

**Decision:** Pitch follows the finest pixel. A pixel with Mercator edge vectors a, b has widths wx = |a×b| / (|ay| + |by|) and wy = |a×b| / (|ax| + |bx|), a box assumed inside its footprint. Base pitches are px, py = 0.98 × the minimum wx, wy over sampled pixels wholly within ±`MAX_LAT`; samples are every 16 px in both axes plus each tile's last row and column. Each tile's cells are kx·px by ky·py, with kx, ky the largest powers of two that fit its own sampled minimum, so all grids nest. A tile also paints cells touching its footprint whose centre lies in a neighbour with smaller cells, using its nearest pixel. The index becomes Uint16 plus a validity bitmask.

**Consequences:** No dependency. Every raster pixel within ±`MAX_LAT` contains a cell centre of its own tile; a pixel straddling the limit may not show. Where tile cell sizes step by 2× or more, up to one coarse cell is drawn twice (accepted). Overlays cost 6.125 B/px on the CPU plus 4 B/px of GPU texture, and are typically 260-300 px a side (~0.4-0.53 MiB). At the cap that is ~101-135 MiB, or ~245 MiB for high-latitude EPSG:4326 tiles (256 x 640 px); there is no hard ceiling. Opening projects about 3 points per sample: an estimated 2.5 s for a 20,480 px square UTM raster, scaling with pixel count (from a measured 0.4 s for 845k projections). Hairlines from MapLibre's per-source corner rounding remain (maplibre-gl 6.16.0 `image_source.ts`; 10 of 1008 seam samples). A custom WebGL layer could remove them later.
